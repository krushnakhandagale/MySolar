How MySolar Works

A seamless 4-step process designed to give your solar panels the care they need without hassle or delay.

01
Step One

Book Service Online

Takes less than 2 minutes

Visit our booking page, fill in your property location, select the estimated number of solar panels, and choose your preferred date and time slot. No advance payments required for V1!

02
Step Two

Cleaner Assigned & Confirmed

Smart matching & quick dispatch

Our automated system assigns a certified solar cleaning technician nearest to your address. You receive an instant SMS or phone call confirming your schedule slot.

03
Step Three

Solar Panels Cleaned Safely

Pure water & non-abrasive tools

Our technician arrives equipped with harness safety gear, de-ionized pure water filtration units, and soft nylon brushes. Your panels are scrubbed and rinsed spotless without chemical risk.

04
Step Four

Service Completed & Efficiency Restored

Enjoy peak energy generation

The cleaning is finalized, a post-wash visual inspection is performed, and you immediately enjoy restored solar efficiency and cleaner clean energy production!
