Pure Water Technology

Why We Never Use Tap Water or Soap

Tap water contains hard minerals like calcium and magnesium that leave spots when dried in sunlight. Soaps leave sticky residues that attract dirt faster.

At MySolar, our 100% Deionized Pure Water Filtration System dissolves grime on contact and dries spotless, leaving glass crystal clear for optimal solar absorption.
