About MySolar

A technology-focused solar cleaning startup designed to make solar panel maintenance simple, reliable, and easy to book.

Re-imagining Solar Maintenance For Modern Solar Owners

As clean solar energy adoption explodes globally, millions of solar panel owners lose 15% to 30% of their power generation simply due to accumulated dust, dirt, and pollution.

Traditional cleaning options were clunky, unreliable, and difficult to schedule. MySolar was founded as a modern technology startup to solve this exact problem — combining quick online booking with certified pure water cleaning specialists.
